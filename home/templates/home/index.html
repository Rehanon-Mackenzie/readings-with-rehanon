About Me

A few years ago I found myself at a crossroads and leaning into astrology and tarot at a much deeper level as another means of assessing who I was and what I wanted. I trained as an evolutionary astrologer and began learning about tarot as a practical tool for self-development. I love this take on spiritual guidance because it really give folks a base to find out about the gifts and growth areas in their chart or the cards and provides practical steps on how to flow with life rather than against it.

Understanding your astrology or consulting the tarot for me is no different to checking the weather report before you head out for the day. Of course we can never be sure it is definitely going to rain but you will not regret packing your umbrella just in case.

I can't wait to help you unpick your own chart or tackle your queries through tarot. Together we can truly work out who you are and what you came here for. My approach is light-hearted and down to earth but provides a clear roadmap to your best chapter yet.
